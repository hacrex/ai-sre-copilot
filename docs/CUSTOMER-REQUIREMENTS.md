# Customer Requirements

## Simulated Customer: Acme SaaS

Acme operates a Kubernetes-based SaaS platform and wants faster incident investigation.

### Requirements

- Detect service degradation from alerts.
- Correlate Kubernetes state with metrics and logs.
- Identify recent deployment changes.
- Provide evidence-backed hypotheses.
- Generate a concise incident report.
- Recommend remediation steps.
- Keep production actions read-only unless explicitly approved.
- Support cloud-neutral Kubernetes deployments.

### Success Metrics

- Reduce mean time to investigate (MTTI).
- Reduce time to produce incident reports.
- Improve consistency of troubleshooting.
- Make evidence and reasoning auditable.
